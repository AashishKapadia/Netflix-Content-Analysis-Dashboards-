# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis Dashboards

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores Netflix’s global content library (Movies & TV Shows) from **1940–2021** using the Kaggle dataset.  
The analysis was performed with **Python (Pandas)** for data cleaning, **Tableau** for visualization, and documented in **Microsoft Word**.

Key insights:
- Netflix library is **97.2% Movies** and **2.8% TV Shows**.
- **United States & India** dominate content contributions (~64% combined).
- **TV-MA** is the most common rating (5,019 titles).
- Content additions peaked in **2018** (~1,852 movies).
- **Dramas & International Movies** lead genres (~3,000 titles each).
- Most movies fall in the **90–119 min runtime sweet spot**.
- **72% of TV shows** end after just 1 season.

📈 Conclusions
* Netflix is a movie-first platform.

* Mature content dominates ratings.

* US & India drive most of the catalog.

* Peak growth was in 2018, followed by decline due to COVID & streaming wars.

* Genres like Drama, International, Comedy dominate, while Kids, Horror, Sci-Fi are underrepresented.

---

✅ Recommendations
* Expand TV show catalog (currently only 2.8%).

* Invest more in kids content.

* Grow African & Latin American representation.

* Strengthen Horror & Sci-Fi offerings.

* Focus on 90–119 min movies (viewer sweet spot).
